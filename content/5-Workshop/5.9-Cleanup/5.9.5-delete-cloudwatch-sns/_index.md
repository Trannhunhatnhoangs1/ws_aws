Finally, delete the monitoring and notification resources (if created) during the practice session.

## Step 1: Delete CloudWatch Alarm

1. Go to **Amazon CloudWatch** → **Alarms**.
2. Delete the created Alarms.

>![Figure 1](/images/5-Workshop/5.10/delete-monitor/alarm.png)

## Step 2: Delete Amazon SNS Topic

1. Go to **Amazon SNS** → **Topics**.
2. Delete the SNS Topic related to the project.

>![Figure 2](/images/5-Workshop/5.10/delete-monitor/topic.png)

At this point, all AWS resources for the workshop have been completely cleaned up.
