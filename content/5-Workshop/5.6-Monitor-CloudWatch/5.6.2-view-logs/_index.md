CloudWatch Logs stores runtime logs generated by the deployed SageMaker Endpoint.

These logs are useful for debugging prediction errors and monitoring endpoint behavior.

## Step 1

Open

```
CloudWatch

→ Log groups
```

> **Figure 1**
>![Figure 1](/images/5-Workshop/5.8/view-logs/log-groups.png)

## Step 2

Select the log group corresponding to your endpoint.

> **Figure 2**

>![Figure 1](/images/5-Workshop/5.8/view-logs/log-group.png)
