After deploying and testing the SageMaker Endpoint, it is important to continuously monitor its performance and operational status.

Amazon CloudWatch automatically collects metrics and logs from SageMaker Endpoints, allowing administrators to observe endpoint health, detect abnormal behavior, and troubleshoot issues quickly.

In this section, you will learn how to:

- View endpoint performance metrics.
- Analyze endpoint logs.
- Create CloudWatch Alarms for automatic monitoring.
