In this section, you will configure Amazon Simple Notification Service (Amazon SNS) to receive automatic email notifications whenever a CloudWatch Alarm is triggered.

Amazon SNS is a fully managed messaging service that delivers notifications to subscribers through multiple communication channels, such as email, SMS, and HTTPS endpoints.

By integrating CloudWatch Alarms with Amazon SNS, administrators can immediately receive alerts when abnormal conditions occur, allowing them to respond quickly and maintain system reliability.

After completing this section, you will learn how to:

- Create an Amazon SNS Topic.
- Subscribe an email address to the topic.
- Test the notification service.
