To receive notifications, subscribe an email address to the SNS Topic.

## Step 1

Open the created SNS Topic.

Click

```
Create subscription
```

> **Figure 1**

>![Figure 1](/images/5-Workshop/5.9/subscribe-email/new.png)

---

## Step 2

Configure

```
Protocol

Email
```

Enter your email address.

> **Figure 2**

>![Figure 2](/images/5-Workshop/5.9/subscribe-email/new.png)

---

## Step 3

Click

```
Create subscription
```

Open your mailbox and confirm the subscription.

> **Figure 3**

>![Figure 3](/images/5-Workshop/5.9/subscribe-email/confirm-email.png)

After confirmation, the subscription status changes to **Confirmed**.
