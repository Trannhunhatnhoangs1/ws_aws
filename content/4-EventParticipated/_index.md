During my internship, I had the opportunity to attend three events organized by AWS: AWS Study Group Community Sharing Session, Cloud Architect - Event GameDay, and FCAJ-Agentic AI BUILD WEEK. These events provided me with practical knowledge in Cloud Computing, Agentic AI, and real-world system architecture design. By listening to industry experts, observing competitive rounds, and networking with professionals, I expanded my perspective on career pathways while solidifying my technical foundation and hands-on learning mindset.

---

### [Event 1](4.1-Event1/)
**Event Name:** AWS Study Group Community Sharing Session

**Time:** 09:00 AM, June 13, 2026

**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Attendee

**Description:** A meetup session focusing on career orientation in Cloud, DevOps, AI, and Data Analytics, while introducing practical skills, real-world experiences, and corporate working culture.

**Key Takeaways:** Through the event, I gained a clearer understanding of career development roadmaps, strengthened my Cloud Computing knowledge, and boosted my motivation to pursue continuous learning and technical skill enhancement.

### [Event 2](4.2-Event2/)

### [Event 3](4.3-Event3/)

**Event Name:** FCAJ-Agentic AI BUILD WEEK

**Time:** 09:00 AM, July 25, 2026

**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Sai Gon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

**Role:** Attendee

**Description:** The event showcased Agentic AI solutions utilizing AWS technologies such as Amazon Bedrock, SageMaker, and Kinesis. Featured projects included a real-time crowd density monitoring and hazard alert system (S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D), an AI assistant for Solution Architects to automate architecture design and cost estimation, and an investigation system for gathering corporate restructuring signals (Signal Scout).

**Key Takeaways:** Through the event, I reinforced my knowledge of Agentic AI and AWS cloud services, gained a deeper understanding of the end-to-end product development lifecycle from ideation to deployment, and learned valuable system architecture approaches and teamwork strategies from the participating teams.
