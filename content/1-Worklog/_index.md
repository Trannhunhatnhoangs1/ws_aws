**On this page**, you will need to introduce your worklog. **How** did you complete it? How many weeks did you take to complete the program? **What** did you do in those weeks?

Typically, and as a standard, a worklog is carried out over about 3 months (throughout the internship period) with weekly contents as follows:

**Week 1:** [Getting familiar with AWS and basic AWS services](1.1-Week1/)

**Week 2:** [Data Preprocessing and Feature Engineering](1.2-Week2/)

**Week 3:** [Local Machine Learning Model Development](1.3-Week3/)

**Week 4:** [Model Evaluation and Hyperparameter Optimization](1.4-Week4/)

**Week 5:** [Model Training on Amazon SageMaker](1.5-Week5/)

**Week 6:** [Deploying the SageMaker Endpoint and Building the Prediction Service](1.6-Week6/)

**Week 7:** [System Integration and End-to-End Testing](1.7-Week7/)

**Week 8:** [System Monitoring, Documentation, and Project Finalization](1.8-Week8/)
